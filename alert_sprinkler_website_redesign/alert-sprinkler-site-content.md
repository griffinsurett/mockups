### FAQ Section

**Section Label:** GOT QUESTIONS
**Section Title:** Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What areas do you service?**
A: We are licensed to operate throughout New Jersey and southeastern Pennsylvania. We've been serving both states since 1977.

**Q: How often does my fire sprinkler system need to be inspected?**
A: NFPA 25 requires inspections on a quarterly, semi-annual, and annual basis depending on the system components. We'll put together a compliant schedule specific to your facility.

**Q: Are you licensed and insured?**
A: Yes. We hold NJ License #P01629 and NJ Fire Safety Certification #195457. We carry full general liability and workers' compensation coverage.

**Q: Do you handle emergency repairs?**
A: Yes — our rapid-response teams are available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Call us at 732-521-1666 any time.

**Q: What types of buildings do you work on?**
A: We serve commercial, industrial, institutional, and residential facilities — from high-rises and warehouses to hospitals, schools, and multi-family housing.

**Q: Can you retrofit an existing building with a sprinkler system?**
A: Absolutely. Retrofitting existing structures is one of our core specialties. Our engineers will assess your building and design a system that meets current code requirements.
